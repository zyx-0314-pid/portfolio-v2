WRITING

NOTES

Learning notes, technical documentation, and older event experiences revisited after reflection.

01

Editorial scope

Three kinds of record.

  1. 01

    Learning notes

    Concepts I am studying, questions I am working through, and explanations written to test whether I understand the subject clearly.

    Study / Reflection

  2. 02

    GitHub documentation

    Context behind public repositories: constraints, setup decisions, architecture, and the reasoning that is difficult to capture in code alone.

    Build / Explain

  3. 03

    Event retrospectives

    What remained useful after an event, what I applied afterward, and which ideas still held up once the immediate excitement had passed.

    Attend / Revisit

02

Publication rule

Events wait six months.

I do not publish an event retrospective until at least six full calendar months after the event date.

This delay creates distance from announcements and first impressions. A retrospective should record what proved useful over time, not simply what happened in the room.

Minimum delay
Six full calendar months
Date shown
Event date and publication date
Focus
Application, changed thinking, and lasting value

03

Archive

Published notes.

No entries published

The archive begins when the first note is ready. No draft titles, event details, or repository claims are displayed before they can be supported.
